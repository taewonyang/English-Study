I barely got up this morning.  오늘 겨우 (간신히) 일어났어.
I almost couldn't get up this morning.  오늘 아침에 거의 못 일어날 뻔했다. 
= I almost slept late this morning.  (slept late = 늦잠자다.(o) // 늦게자다(x) )

[make it to +어디 : 어디에 제시간에 도착하다.]  ex) make it to school // make it to meeting.
I almost couldn't make it to work on time  오늘 제시간에 출근을 못할뻔 했어.
= I was almost late to work today   [be late to ~:  ~어디에 늦다.]

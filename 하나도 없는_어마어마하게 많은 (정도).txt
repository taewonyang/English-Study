1. 하나도 없는   not have any  //  have no
ex) I don't have any questions. : 질문이 전혀 없습니다.
I have no regrets.  : 저는 후회가 하나도 없습니다.
I have no doubt about it  : 의심의 여지가 전혀 없어요.  (=확실해요)

2. 거의 없는  have almost no  //   barely have any   (barely : 겨우)
ex) I have almost no regrets.  : 저는 후회가 거의 하나도 없어요.
They had almost no plants in their office.  : 그들의 사무실 안에 식물이 거의 없었어요.
I have almost no money saved.  :  저는 모아둔 돈이 거의 하나도 없어요.
It was weird. He barely had any photos in his phone.  이상했어. 그는 휴대폰에 사진이 거의 없었어.
I barely have any memory of childhood.  :  저는 어렸을 때의 기억이 거의 없어요.


3. 얼마 없는   have (very) little (불가산) /  few (가산)
   얼마 밖에 없다.  -  only have  /  have only  (가산에서만 쓰임)
I have very little time. (시간)
I have very littel money. (돈)
he has very little experience (경험)
We have very little knowledge about that. (지식)
I have very few friends. 
I only have a few hours.
we only have a few things to do today.

4. 많지 않다. not have many (much / a lot)
They don't have a lot of customers today.  ( many customers)
She doesn't have much interest in that sort of thing.

5. 좀 있는  have some / have a few
I have some ideas.
We had a few arguments on our way back.

6. 꽤 많은 - have quite a few /  have quite a lot.
I have quite a few hats.  (가산)
He had quite a few drinks last night.  (가산)
We have quite a lot of extra room(space).   (불가산)

7. 많은  have a lot of
we have a lot (of things) in common.  : 공통점이 많습니다.

8. 정말 많은  have so many / have so much
I have so much work to do.
He always has so many questions about everything

9. 어마어마하게 많은   have a huge amount(number) of
They have a huge amount of debt.  (불가산)
We had a huge number of complaints about this last year. (가산)

10. 너무 많은 (그래서 문제가 되는)  - have too many(much)
My trainer says that I have too much body fat.

extent : 규모, 정도
to some extent : 어느 정도까지
ex) I agree with you to some extent.

beyond a certain point
: 어느 정도를 넘어서면
ex) Beyond a certain point, studying more isn't helpful.

place order  

go for  ~ 택하다. 
ex) Usually in the morning, I go for black coffee.

cut back on ~ 줄이다.
I'm also trying to cut back on caffeine.
so I'll go for decaf.

go to drink 주로 마시는 커피
ex) my go-to drink is an iced latte.

take a sip : 한 모금 마시다.

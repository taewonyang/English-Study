2. (사물+사물)  [A goes well with B] [match ]

- go well with  -
너 머리 스타일이 오늘 옷차림이랑 잘 어울린다.
ex) Your hairstyle goes (really) well with your outfit today.
둘이 잘 맞을 거라고 생각하지 않았어요.
ex) I didn't expect them to work well together
- match -
ex) I wore slacks to see if shoes matched them.
ex) I didn’t expect a bitter drink and a sweet dessert to match so well.

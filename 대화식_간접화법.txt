======= Part2. 설명식 (간접화법) =========
걔가 점심을 먹으로 나가는 길이었다고 맬했어. 그리고 나보고 같이 가겠냐고 물어봤어.
She said she was on her way to lunch and asked me if I wanted to come along. 
근데 내가 뭘 좀 하고 있어서 좀 기다려 줄 수 있냐고 말했어.
I said I was in the middle of something and asked her if she could wait for me a little.
그랬더니 아랫층에 가 있겠다고 그랬어.
She said she would be downstairs. 
And I told her I would be there as soon as I could.
She told me I didn't need to hurry.


~ 에 가는 길이다.
* be on <누구의> way (to) + <어디>
ex) be on my way home.
I'm on my way to school.  //  I'm on my way to work.
